Setup guide — do this once

  1. Step 1: Click Download Vault Structure below — saves 5 files (hub + 4 category notes)
  2. Step 2: Create a folder in your Obsidian vault called Al Laith Marketing Brain
  3. Step 3: Drag all 5 files into that folder
  4. Step 4: In Obsidian Graph View settings → Groups → add tag:#mastered (green), tag:#studying (amber), tag:#not-started (grey)
  5. After that: Export individual concepts as you learn them — they'll link into the structure automatically
