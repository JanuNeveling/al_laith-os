# Al Laith — Brand Assets Spec
_Locked-in source of truth for Content Creation Hub theming and any in-app brand-aligned outputs._

## Logo
Three approved variants. Use based on background:

| Variant | When to use | Filename (target) |
|---|---|---|
| Black-on-transparent | Light backgrounds | `logo-black.png` |
| White-on-transparent | Dark backgrounds, photography overlays | `logo-white.png` |
| Navy-on-transparent | Mid-tone or brand-coloured backgrounds | `logo-navy.png` (uses `#183057`) |

Place final PNG/SVG files in: `Content Creation/Brand_Assets/logos/`

| Hex | Role |
|---|---|

| `#72747E` | Brand grey (neutral / supporting text) |

| Hex | Role |
|---|---|

### Shades
| Hex | Role |
|---|---|
| `#0082AF` | Darker shade of primary — pressed states, accents |

---

## Typography
**Arial** — primary brand typeface. Used for all UI, body, headings.
- Headings: Arial Bold
- Body: Arial Regular
- Numerics / data: Arial (tabular figures where supported)
